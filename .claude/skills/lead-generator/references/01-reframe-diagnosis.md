# Phase 0 — Reframe & Diagnose

Only use this when the user reports outbound failure ("no leads", "no replies", "I've tried everything", "my offer isn't landing").

## Step 1 — Validate (don't skip)

Say, literally, in your own words:

> You're not lazy. You're not imagining it. Waking up to no leads, no replies, a silent inbox — that's a real, specific kind of pain. Before we change anything, I want to name that.

This is not fluff. If you skip validation and jump to "fix your ICP", the user will resist because they feel judged. Pain → frustration → validation → openness. That's the sequence.

## Step 2 — Reframe

Exactly one sentence:

> The problem is almost never motivation. It's system mismatch. One of seven parts of your system is wrong, and more effort on a broken system still produces zero.

The seven parts:

| # | System part      | What "broken" looks like                                           |
|---|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Prospect         | You target people who don't have the pain                          |
| 2 | Timing           | You target people whose pain hasn't crossed the threshold yet      |
| 3 | Signal           | You target by job title, not by active buying signal               |
| 4 | Offer            | The value prop takes more than 5 seconds to understand             |
| 5 | Message          | Opens with "Hi, we help..." — indistinguishable from every cold DM |
| 6 | CTA              | First ask is a 30-min call — too big, too early                    |
| 7 | Feedback loop    | You don't track which angle works, so you can't improve            |

## Step 3 — Seven-question diagnostic

Ask the user these in order. Single-line answers are fine.

1. Who specifically are you messaging? (title + industry + company size)
2. What makes you believe *they* have the pain *right now*?
3. What observable signal tells you they're in motion (hiring, funding, public frustration, failed attempts)?
4. Can you state your offer in one sentence: "I help [X] get [Y] without [Z]"?
5. Does your opener mention something true about *that specific prospect*?
6. What is your first CTA? (the literal text)
7. Do you have a log of: messages sent, replies received, which angle each was?

## Step 4 — Map answers to fix

| Question failed | Go to phase | Reason                         |
|-----------------|-------------|--------------------------------|
| 1               | Phase 1     | ICP is fuzzy                   |
| 2, 3            | Phase 2     | No timing signal               |
| 4               | Phase 5     | Offer has cognitive load       |
| 5               | Phase 5     | Message is noise, not signal   |
| 6               | Phase 5     | CTA is too big                 |
| 7               | Phase 6     | No feedback loop               |

If multiple fail, fix in order 1 → 7. Prospect and timing dominate everything downstream.

## Script-level phrases to reuse

- "If your message blends into every other cold DM, the brain files it as noise."
- "Doing nothing feels free to the prospect. Your message has to make inaction feel expensive."
- "Big asks die. Micro-yeses compound."
- "Every reply, ignore, and drop-off is a data point. Ignoring that is throwing away free intel."
