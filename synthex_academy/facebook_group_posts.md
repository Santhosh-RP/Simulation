## Posting Tips

1. **Use line breaks generously** — Facebook posts read best with short paragraphs.
2. **Lead with the pain, not the brand** — people scroll past company names.
3. **Add 1–2 emojis maximum per post** — too many feels spammy.
4. **Post at peak times** — 7–9 AM, 12–1 PM, 7–10 PM UK time.
5. **Engage with every comment** — Facebook's algorithm rewards comment volume.
6. **Don't post the same copy in multiple groups within the same hour** — Facebook flags this as spam.
7. **Rotate between Posts 1, 2, 3, and 4** across the week to avoid repetition.
