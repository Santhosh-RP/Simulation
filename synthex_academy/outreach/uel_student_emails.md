## ⚠️ Before You Send

1. **GDPR / PECR** — If these contacts didn't opt in to marketing, cold outreach to UK individuals carries legal risk. Safer path: position the first email as a **personal, non-automated** message from you (a real person), keep it low-volume, and include a clear opt-out line.
2. **Sender** — Use a real personal address (e.g. you@synthex-academy.com), not noreply.
3. **Volume** — Send in small batches (25–50/day) from a warmed inbox or deliverability drops.
4. **Opt-out** — Always include "Reply UNSUBSCRIBE and I'll remove you" at the bottom.
5. **Subject lines** — Keep under 50 characters; no ALL CAPS; no spammy words like "FREE!!!".

```

"qualified" to "shortlisted" isn't about your degree — it's about how you're
positioning it on a UK CV.
```

```
Applying to UK roles from outside the UK is 10x harder — UK recruiters screen
out non-UK CV formats in seconds. That's exactly the gap we close: UK-
standard CV, UK-tone LinkedIn, and a targeted application strategy that
actually gets you shortlisted.
```

```
You've been part of the UEL community for years — you've built real
experience. The reason interviews aren't coming isn't your background; it's
that your CV probably isn't framing it the way UK recruiters need to see it.
```
